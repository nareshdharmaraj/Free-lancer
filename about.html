Our Story

From humble beginnings to global excellence

From a Single Desk in Brooklyn
to a Global Powerhouse

LexIcon was founded on a simple principle: excellence is not negotiable. What began as a one-person operation in 1985 has grown into one of the world's most respected legal institutions, representing Fortune 500 companies and high-net-worth individuals across three continents.

Our Journey

Four decades of legal excellence

1985

The Foundation

Arthur Vanguard opens a modest practice in Brooklyn, specializing in corporate law. His first case: defending a small manufacturing company against an unjust patent claim. He wins, setting the tone for decades to come.

1995

Expansion & Recognition

After winning a landmark $500M verdict in a securities fraud case, LexIcon attracts national attention. The firm expands to Manhattan, hiring 15 elite attorneys from top law schools.

2005

Global Reach

Opening offices in London and Hong Kong, LexIcon becomes a truly international firm. The firm now handles complex cross-border M&A transactions worth billions.

2010

The Sterling Merger

LexIcon merges with Sterling Partners, a boutique litigation firm known for their trial excellence. The combined entity now boasts 50+ attorneys and wins 94% of cases taken to trial.

2018

Top 100 Recognition

The firm is ranked in the Global Top 100 Law Firms by Legal 500. LexIcon's IP practice wins three landmark cases that shape technology law for the next decade.

2024

Middle East Expansion

Opening a state-of-the-art office in Dubai, LexIcon now serves clients across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. The firm handles over $5 billion in annual case value.
